## Introduction

The purpose of this datasheet is to present the complete design documentation for the **Sprinkler Actuator Subsystem**. It provides a detailed overview of the system’s electrical and mechanical components, including the **schematic**, **block diagram**, **power budget**, and **component selection rationale**. These elements collectively demonstrate the design’s functionality, efficiency, and reliability—highlighting how power is distributed, how control signals are managed, and why specific components were chosen to meet project requirements. 


### Project Summary

The sprinkler actuator subsystem serves as the mechanical output stage of Team 101’s Water Efficient Lawn Watering System, responsible for physically controlling water flow to the plants. It is driven by an Adafruit 711 DC motor powered through a FAN8100N H-bridge, allowing precise bidirectional control from the PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano microcontroller. The subsystem uses a 9 V, 3 A power supply, regulated as needed for stable operation, ensuring sufficient torque and responsiveness to actuate the sprinkler valve efficiently. This design highlights effective integration of mechanical motion control with electronic regulation, forming a reliable and energy-efficient solution for automated irrigation.
